# X100 Product FAQ

## First Use

Before first use, charge X100 for at least 30 minutes, confirm the status indicator is steady white, remove the transport lock, and run the self-check from the mobile app. If the app reports fault code E01 during first use, stop setup and follow the cannot-start SOP.

## Cleaning

Use a dry microfiber cloth for routine cleaning. A 70% alcohol wipe may be used on the outer shell only. Do not pour liquid into vents, ports, buttons, or the charging base.

## Accessories

X100 supports the X100-C1 charging base, X100-F2 filter kit, and X100-B1 battery pack. Accessories for X90 are not supported because the latch and voltage profile are different.

## Connectivity

X100 supports 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and Bluetooth pairing. It does not support 5 GHz-only Wi-Fi networks. If setup fails with N04, ask the customer to confirm the router band and retry pairing near the router.

## Noise

A low fan sound during startup is normal for up to 20 seconds. Grinding, clicking, or vibration after startup should be treated as an abnormal noise case and checked against the E17 SOP.
