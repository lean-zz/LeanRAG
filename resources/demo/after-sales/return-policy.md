# X100 Return And Exchange Policy

## Return Window

Customers may request return within 7 calendar days after delivery when the product is unused, complete, and in resale condition. Enterprise bulk orders follow the signed contract if it differs from this policy.

## Exchange Window

Customers may request exchange within 15 calendar days after delivery for confirmed quality issues. The inspection report must identify a manufacturing or logistics defect.

## Non-Returnable Cases

Return may be rejected when the device is missing accessories, has unauthorized disassembly marks, has liquid ingress, has impact damage, or was used beyond normal inspection.

## Inspection Flow

Support agents should collect order number, serial number, delivery date, issue description, photos or videos, and packaging status. A service center or warehouse inspector makes the final return or exchange decision.

## Refund Timing

Approved refunds are submitted within 3 business days after warehouse acceptance. Bank or payment-platform settlement time may vary.
