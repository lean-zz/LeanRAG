# X100 Service Center Policy

## Supported Cities

The demo service-center lookup supports Shanghai, Beijing, Shenzhen, and Chengdu. Other cities should be handled by mail-in repair unless a contract service partner is configured.

## Shanghai

Shanghai service center accepts X100 inspection and repair by appointment. Standard inspection lead time is 1 business day. Standard repair lead time is 3 to 5 business days after quotation approval.

## Beijing

Beijing service center accepts X100 inspection, warranty replacement, and paid repair by appointment. Standard repair lead time is 3 to 6 business days.

## Shenzhen

Shenzhen service center handles X100 batch inspection, accessory replacement, and enterprise customer appointments. Standard repair lead time is 2 to 5 business days.

## Chengdu

Chengdu service center handles X100 inspection and mail-in pickup coordination. Standard repair lead time is 4 to 7 business days.

## Appointment Rules

Customers should provide product model, serial number, fault symptom, contact phone, and preferred appointment date. Safety cases with smoke or burning smell must be escalated before appointment confirmation.
