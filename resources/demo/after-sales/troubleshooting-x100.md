# X100 Troubleshooting SOP

## E01 Cannot Start

Likely causes: low battery, transport lock not removed, charger mismatch, firmware boot failure, or mainboard protection.

Customer checks: charge for 30 minutes with the X100-C1 charging base, remove the transport lock, hold the power button for 8 seconds, and confirm whether the indicator flashes amber.

Support-agent checks: confirm serial number, purchase date, charger model, firmware version, and whether the issue started after a fall or liquid exposure.

Escalation conditions: no indicator after charging, repeated E01 after reset, burning smell, swollen battery, or visible liquid ingress.

## E17 Abnormal Motor Noise

Likely causes: foreign object, fan imbalance, worn bearing, unsupported filter, or impact damage.

Customer checks: power off, remove the filter kit, inspect for visible debris, reinstall X100-F2 filter, and test for 10 seconds.

Support-agent checks: ask for a short noise video and verify whether unsupported X90 accessories were installed.

Escalation conditions: grinding sound after reinstall, vibration, smoke, or customer reports the device was dropped.

## E37 Display Abnormality

Likely causes: screen cable fault, firmware display driver issue, low-temperature startup, or panel damage.

Customer checks: restart the device, keep it at room temperature for 20 minutes, and check whether the mobile app can still connect.

Support-agent checks: collect firmware version, photo of the display, and whether the screen is black, flickering, or showing E37.

Escalation conditions: black screen after restart, cracked panel, repeated E37, or failed app connection.

## N04 Network Pairing Failure

Likely causes: 5 GHz-only router, weak signal, wrong Wi-Fi password, VPN isolation, or Bluetooth permission disabled.

Customer checks: use 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, move within 2 meters of the router, enable Bluetooth permission, and retry pairing.

Support-agent checks: confirm router band, phone OS, app version, and whether enterprise network isolation blocks pairing.

Escalation conditions: repeated N04 on two phones and two networks, or enterprise network requires allowlist support.
