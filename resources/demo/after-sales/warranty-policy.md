# X100 Warranty And Repair Policy

## Standard Coverage

X100 has a 24-month standard warranty from the purchase date. The battery pack has a 12-month warranty. Accessories sold separately have a 6-month warranty unless the invoice states a longer period.

## Required Proof

Warranty service requires at least one of: valid invoice, order number, enterprise purchase contract, or serial number matched to a registered purchase record.

## Covered Cases

Manufacturing defects, normal-use component failure, firmware-caused startup failure, and confirmed sensor module failure are covered during the warranty period.

## Exclusions

Accidental water damage, unauthorized disassembly, impact damage, use of unsupported accessories, and damage caused by non-approved chargers are not covered by standard warranty.

## Out-Of-Warranty Repair

Out-of-warranty devices can still be repaired after inspection. The service center must provide a quotation before paid repair. Customers may decline paid repair after inspection and only pay logistics fees where applicable.

## Escalation

Escalate safety, injury, smoke, burning smell, privacy, legal, or repeated-repair cases to senior support.
